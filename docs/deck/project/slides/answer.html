What RivalLens does

One loop, from a page change to a pricing decision

1 · Watch

Finds India-serving competitors and saves their pricing pages

2 · Filter

Ignores noise and lists every ignored difference with its reason

3 · Prove

A receipt per change: saved pages, highlighted value, dates

4 · Decide

Ranked alert, comparison, and a launch price that re-plans

Detection is rules-first and deterministic. The AI drafts profiles, suggests competitors and summarises reports, but never decides what changed.

The four steps are the product. The key design choice: the LLM never decides whether something changed. That is rules plus evidence, so it is repeatable and explainable to a judge.
