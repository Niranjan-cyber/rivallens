Detection method

Section hashes, then rules, then a field diff

Each page is split into sections (nav, footer, banners, each block of main) and hashed three ways: normalised text, raw text, HTML.

Prices are read only inside pricing sections, with plausibility checks learned from real pages ($829,000 ROI stats and “monthly spend” surveys are dropped).

Suspicious moves (over 80%, vanished price, new currency) are kept for review and never alert.

Changed sectionLedger reason
nav, header, footer, asidenavigation_or_footer
banner, promo, cookiepromo_banner
HTML only, same textformatting_only
spacing or case onlywhitespace
same lines, new orderreordering
digits only, outside pricingdynamic_content
pricing or headlinefield diff → change
Every changed section lands in exactly one bucket: the ledger or a change. That is why the cosmetic redesign gives 4 ledger rows and 0 changes, and the price cut gives 1 change. Both are unit tests on the real rendered mock pages.
