The problem

Founders stop trusting competitor alerts, because most of them are noise.

Alert fatigue

Page-change tools fire on a reordered menu, a new banner, a CSS tweak or a clock.

No proof

“They cut their price” is a claim. Without the before and after, nobody acts on it.

No memory

A one-off SWOT report can’t say what changed last week, or what to do about it.

False alerts are the most cited complaint about page-change tools (our research: Visualping reviews). And a one-off AI competitor report, which is what most teams build for this problem statement, has no memory and no evidence.
