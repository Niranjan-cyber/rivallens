# Kesar Bakehouse demo exports (demo data, fictional business)

Kesar Bakehouse is a fictional bakery café in Pune. Its files are **real data**, re-dated and re-priced; nothing in them is invented.

| File | What it is | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| `orders-apr2026-aug2026.csv` | POS item export: Order Date, Order ID, Item, Quantity, Unit Price (INR); 14,169 items | Every transaction of **The Bread Basket**, a real Edinburgh bakery (CC0 Public Domain; OpenML dataset 43422, Kaggle `mittalvasu95/the-bread-basket`), for its five complete months Nov 2016 – Mar 2017, moved forward 113 months to Apr – Aug 2026. Each item is priced at a comparable item on **Theobroma's public online menu** (theobroma.in, a Mumbai and Pune bakery chain), read on 24 Oct 2026 with the app's robots.txt-respecting fetcher: e.g. Coffee → Cappuccino [250ml] ₹175, Brownie → Choco Chip Brownie ₹110, Cake → Rich Chocolate Pastry ₹155. 13 items have a comparable; the rest (Medialuna, Cookies, Scone…) are dropped. Generated by `api/scripts/bakery_data.py` (deterministic) |
| `reviews-amazon-fine-food-baked-goods.csv` | 60 customer reviews of baked goods | **Amazon Fine Food Reviews** (SNAP, CC0; Hugging Face mirror `PJ2005/amazon-fine-food-reviews`, Apache-2.0) |

There is no bank statement, so costs, margin and runway show as Unknown until the business adds them.
