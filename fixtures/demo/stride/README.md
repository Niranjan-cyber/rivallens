# Stride & Co. demo exports (demo data, fictional business)

Stride & Co. is a fictional footwear D2C brand in Pune. Its exports are **modelled on real data**, not invented. They were generated by `api/scripts/demo_data.py`, which is deterministic.

| File | What it is | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| `orders-oct2025-sep2026.xlsx` | Store order export: Order ID, Order Date, Product, Quantity, Unit Price (INR); 3,442 orders | The order rhythm of **UCI Online Retail II** (Chen, D. 2012, doi:10.24432/C5CG6D, CC BY 4.0). One in 7 real invoices from Dec 2010 to Nov 2011, kept with their real dates (moved to Oct 2025 – Sep 2026) and real cancellations (returns). Each invoice is mapped to one Stride product at a fixed price: Runner ₹1,999, Trail ₹2,499, Socks ₹299 |
| `bank-statement-oct2025-sep2026.csv` | HDFC-style statement: Date, Narration, Withdrawal Amt., Deposit Amt., Closing Balance | Stock costs are 46.5% of each month's revenue, from **Campus Activewear FY26** gross margin of 53.5% (reported results). Rent is ₹65,000 and 6 salaries total ₹2,40,000 a month; Meta ads are 18% and shipping 8% of revenue; there's one ₹50 L angel round. Razorpay settlements equal each month's order revenue |

Upload both files on the Money dashboard. Revenue is counted from the order export, and bank settlements for the same months are recognised as the same money.
